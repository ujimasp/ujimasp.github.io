Prospective Students

Thinking About Joining the Lab?

We're always excited to meet students who are passionate about our mission. Before reaching out, please read all of the information at the links below carefully and follow the instructions provided — this applies to PhD applicants, master's students, undergraduates, and high school students alike.

This step matters more than you might think. Our work requires careful attention to detail, the ability to follow complex processes, and strong self-direction. How you approach contacting us is the first indication of how you'll approach your work with us.

If following instructions feels like an obstacle, this may not be the right fit. If it feels like a reasonable ask — welcome, we'd love to hear from you.

A Note on Letters of Recommendation

We are happy to support students in their academic and professional journeys, and that includes writing letters of recommendation. However, to write a meaningful letter, we need something to write about. Students must complete at least two successful quarters in the lab and make genuine contributions to their assigned project before a letter can be requested.

If part of your reason for joining is to eventually obtain a letter of recommendation, that's completely understandable — we won't hold that against you. What we do ask is that you plan accordingly. A letter written after two strong quarters of real contribution will serve you far better than one written without that foundation.

Join us, do the work, and the letter will write itself.
